Who We Are

For beginners to any level.

CAMS Hack Club is a student-run CS club where you build fun projects, learn new skills, and hang out with people who love making things. No experience required — just curiosity.

What we do

We work on fun, hands-on projects — from games to websites to tools — and learn through doing. Every meeting has something new.

The final project from this workshop — a random number guessing game with hints.

import random secret = random.randint(1, 100) while True: guess = int(input("Guess (1-100): ")) if guess < secret: print("Too low!") elif guess > secret: print("Too high!") else: print("You got it!") break
